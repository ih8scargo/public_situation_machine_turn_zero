The sketch vocabulary informs the overhead bureaucracy, planar turntable,
substantial appliance cabinet, curved rail, operated lever control and capacious
Crossing. COB becomes bodily after Appointmenting, TC comes to stand beneath the
machine after Second, and Third furnishes rugging and a quiet bodily enclosure.

trace supports pointer, touch and arrow keys.

The lever is an explicit atomic Human operation; it does not infer bodily passage
from dragging or measure actual physical Human seating. The Human confirms primitive
SEATED FOR standing. This is the deliberately limited operational proposition being
tested; no mature Traversaling Seat or GREAT BIG RE-STEP is imported.

Tensions deliberately left visible:

1. The old initial Place and this pass's earned destination have incompatible
   ancestry. Versioned read-only witnesses preserve that difference honestly.
2. A Stationing Locality differs from an event: FIRST contains two labors, while
   seating and passage extend Third without new Appointmentings.
3. Completed chains are historical inherited ancestry, not claims that all earlier
   relations remain the current FROM. Passage records and source/after snapshots
   expose the distinction.
4. Primitive seat/STEP controls are declarations of Human labor, not physical
   computer relocation or embodied motion sensing.
5. The on-deck chains deliberately accumulate. No zipper, Pocket, Gallery or mature
   Roundabout mechanism is added to conceal the resulting clutter.
