## Separate Tunnel drive

The Separate Tunnel test link seeds an independent, already-furnished fixture
using the existing calibration/TC/first STEP contracts. It is **not** a shortcut
operation in the public Groundworking sequence. The resulting record has exactly
one first-STEP passage and saved FROM `ONE-APPROACH`. Existing already-STEPped
records can be used instead. No further consequential operation is offered.

1. Hold ALONG. Bobbing gathers pace, bounded at a comfortable prototype maximum.
2. Release. Pace eases rather than stopping immediately. Hold BACK to decelerate
   and reverse. Arrow Up/Down gives equivalent held navigation; Space/Enter on
   the NAV buttons works too. Window blur and hidden-tab events release input.
3. The floor's small repeated NAV tacks make direction and pace visible. They
   have no semantic identities and establish no passage extents.
4. Hold the transverse controls, or press/drag the open floor across, to bias
   the current section toward a side. ACROSS is restrained and settles toward
   the course on release. There is no free-flight camera.
5. Catch a corner of a nearby sheet. Bobbing's articulation takes that corner;
   drawing with the pointer brings the sheet toward Regard. The relation holds
   after the drawing stroke so another corner can be taken up.
6. Catch a second distinct corner. Draw further, or use the now-available Square
   TOWARD Regard stroke. The sheet deliberately becomes head-on and readable.
   Its text can scroll within that held presentation, including touch/keyboard.
   No lucky perspective angle is required.
7. Relinquish (or Escape). The same sheet relaxes to its own side/course standing.
   Resume NAV. No locality, ancestry or seating relation has changed.

For keyboard sheet work, Tab to a visible corner and press Space/Enter. Catch a
second corner, then use the Square stroke or arrow-driven tension. Once square,
focus its writing for ordinary reading/scrolling. Relinquish returns to passing

external nested ellipses or moving camera is used. Two Rail courses, a distinct

the enclosure without being equated. Floor provenance derives from the record's

drawing, tensioning and relinquishing never write material or move its locality.

corner positions, pace marks, tool pose and four-point articulation live in the

steps, deterministic acceleration/friction and a restrained transverse spring.
The actual rendered sheet identity is always a ledger ID; hit geometry does not
manufacture semantic identity. One corner cannot square a sheet; two distinct
