## Compose recovered material

The material ledger below the cabinet edits existing records and registers new
ones. Each item carries identity, revisable language, locality/Tunnel placement,
origin, derived TOWARD, region, multiple responsibilities, bearing/course hints,
explicit dependencies, revision and source history. Language lives in the seed
and persisted ledger, rather than explanatory template panels.

A Tunnel placement must be authored FROM that Tunnel's furnishing locality.
Changing a material inscription does not furnish its consequential dependencies.
All seven locality identities and six intervening Tunnels are registered as
composing conjectures. Material may be filed against any of them; only the first
slice is rendered/driveable. Rail order supplies no implicit dependency.

The canonical Toy Box contains fifteen identities, including the recurring
Dispensationaling Stand with initial furnishing RUGGING. Its shared record editor
supports encounter, Aim/branding, dependencies, Pretendingable/Usingable status,
job, provenance and unresolved notes. Toybox Regard resolves the corresponding
encounter records, not a copied inventory per locality. Usingability still checks
actual dependencies. Later inventory entries are inspectable records and do not
claim implemented apparatus. The airplane is not marked Pretendingable.

## Domain / projection boundary

`Architecture.standing` alone is saved in `experimental_architecture_records`,
with optimistic locking. It includes identities, relations, requirements,
passage ancestry, material and Toy Box records. Existing bench records are not
read or written. Tray/bulb instrumentation has not been merged into this slice.

Human/actual TC are given entities, not manufactured by the calibration operation.
Projected TC, its Chair, Human Place-Square, Inner Aperture, four-pin square and
Slate Base Square have differentiated identities and jobs. Relations carry
subject/predicate/object, origin, dependencies and provenance.

STEP writes FROM `ONE-APPROACH`, with a passage FROM ZEROETH BY-WAY-OF ZEROETH–ONE.
This is a provisional name for consequential admission toward the receiving
locality. It does **not** assert completed ONE, or FROM WITHIN all its future
contents. The inherited relation IDs are recorded in the first passage.

Projectioning-er state, flat mode, Pivot bearing and NAV course are ephemeral.
OFF resets them and does not save them. Neither SVG geometry nor a label may
establish a relation. The two Rail paths derive from one course projection;
the teeth are a visual hook for later tractioning, not a law equating Rail,
Zippering, Road or Semantical Stringing. There is no charged state, airplane
circuit implementation, calibrated RE-STEP stroke or seven-stage wizard.
