## Review recommendation

Retain THROUGH, ALONG THROUGH and THROUGH ALONG with their already-adjudicated
referents. For the present **engineering** trial, speak of a **course**, **inlet**,
**bend purchase**, **continuation**, **staged marker**, and **reversible skating**.
Do not appoint TO, an official Tunnel Piping Segmentation taxonomy, or one
preposition per candidate resolutioning extent merely because the UI can draw it.

The bounded review should decide (1) what boundary supplies THROUGH, (2) what held
segmentation supplies THROUGH ALONG, (3) what course/participant standing warrants
ALONG THROUGH, and (4) what accomplishment could ever distinguish TO from TOWARD.
One reviewed course may support several of these readings; another may lack the
necessary furnishing altogether.

## Appliance/NAV pass — proposed distinctions for review

The [engineering print](machine-instructionings/appliance-cutaway-nav-source.txt)
explicitly distinguishes NAV-STEPPING within one current turn from consequential
RE-STEP. This implementation retains the following experimental distinctions:

- **INTO** the appliance: front Crossing, approach surfaces, and local inward extent;
  a projection relation, not a new Human FROM or an automatic transportation.
- **ALONG THROUGH** the retained course: reversible distance and course-local up;
  no authorship, evaluation, sealing or new turn is inferred from skating.
- **Additional JANUSITE issuance**: explicit Human appointment and finite appended
  material; the earlier course is retained. Issuance alone does not move COB.
- **TOWARD the approaching landinging**: a fixed future seating locality with source
  provenance. Arrival does not establish the future Seat WITH COB or accomplish sitting.
- **Regarding the Outreading**: a second presentation of the same course/locality;
  dismissing, reversing a notice, or changing inspection bearing does not relocate COB.

These are review proposals for this apparatus, not replacements for M12's distinct
Road, Caterpillar and Gallery expressions. The finite return loops are engineering
rehearsals; no constitutional next room or new canonical piping class is appointed.
