### 5. Build the experimental Mouse Treadmill

This is a priority deliverable.

Furnish a small, mechanically legible, circular **mouse-operated purchase surface** in a stable Human-facing control locality.

The Human moves the mouse pointer onto a purchase region, presses, and holds.

While held, that region furnishes a deterministic amount of purchase toward COB's traversal.

Begin with a modest provisional arrangement, such as:

- Rest / no additional purchase.
- Gentle accelerating purchase.
- Working accelerating purchase.
- Stronger accelerating purchase.
- Decelerating purchase.

These are **starting settings, not canonical values**.

The Human should be able to see which region is engaged, the purchase it furnishes, and COB's resulting traversal response.

Distinguish commanded purchase from COB's actual velocity. Provide an explicit, deterministic acceleration/deceleration rule and a documented release/coasting rule.

The mouse cannot physically push back, so represent resistance through detents, visible mechanical engagement, dwell, motion, or differing amounts of gesture needed to obtain purchase.

The regions should be inspectable and their arrangement and strength should be configurable in a simple experimental manner. Do not build a large settings administration system. A compact configuration surface or well-documented local parameters are sufficient for this pass.

Holding a region is an operation of an already-appointed affordmenting. It is not repeated appointmenting.

Releasing the mouse button, leaving the relevant interaction region, or losing browser focus must reliably end active purchase.

Keep this **accelerator distinct from the Regarding Steering Wheel**. The accelerator operates COB's traversal; the Steering Wheel will operate the Human's Regarding.

Do not replace the four-square Regarding apparatus with the final wheel during this bounded pass, but preserve a fixed, usable locality for that imminent recovery.
