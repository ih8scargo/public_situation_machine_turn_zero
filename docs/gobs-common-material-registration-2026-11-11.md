## Inspectable midpoint-to-extent mapping

The actual monitor is rectangular. Its lower-left→upper-right diagonal has midpoint M. Taking the left and right edge midpoints gives the trial transverse endpoints A and B:

```text
Q(t) = A + t(B−A),  0 ≤ t ≤ 1
Q(1/2) = (A+B)/2 = M = (D0+D1)/2
|B−A| = .66 raw model units > 0
```

Thus the point M anchors a nonzero extent. The mapping does not pretend that a point alone determines the extent's length, physical direction or material transformation. A and B come from source geometry, but selecting that existing span as a YT presentation is a hypothesis, explicitly recorded in the ledger. This is neither a recovered hinge nor a measured folding operation.

For a source with N graphemes, source index i is presented at `t=(i+.5)/N`. This is a normalized display address, **not furnishing chronology or emergence timing**. Full text remains readable above the diagrams. At most 80 source samples are retained in the inspection payload, preserving the first/last 40 for longer text; at most the first/last eight markers are drawn for larger samples. Each marker carries the original material ID, index, parameter and world coordinate, with a keyboard-focusable title. Short ribbons can show their actual graphemes. Source truncation for drawing never rewrites the text or ancestry.

The Flat projection is Snail XY: `(x,y,z)→(x,−y)` before screen fitting. Tunneling uses the existing provisional eye E, forward `unit(M−E)`, right `unit(UP×forward)`, and up `forward×right`. Perspective coordinates divide the right/up dot products by positive forward depth. This puts M at the sight center. The incoming bearing M→E projects to the same center, as expected for an axial bearing. It is not rendered as a misleading screen-length transport arrow.

The Tunneling window fits the diagonal/transverse source geometry. The distant XT reference is outside that focused window and is explicitly available in Flat and the ledger. Flat shows the inherited XT line and the trial YT span using one projection. Both projections retain original world coordinates; independent screen fitting is view state, not a physical material transform.

## Independent Ground / upper-boundary check

For Ground point G, nonzero UP vector u, boundary point B and registered model-units-per-extent s, the checker evaluates:

```text
height_in_extents = dot(B−G, u/|u|) / s
residual = height_in_extents − 6
```

It reports CONSISTENT only within numerical tolerance. Unknown G, B or s reports UNREGISTERED; zero UP also reports UNREGISTERED. It never substitutes NEXT's center, assigns its floor, or tests horizontal center distance as SIX.

Tests include a boundary with arbitrary horizontal offset and exactly SIX vertical extents, a SIX-horizontal/zero-height failure, a rotated UP frame, and missing/zero-direction inputs. The **actual current scene check remains UNREGISTERED**, since neither the shared Ground nor NEXT upper boundary is supplied. These mathematical controls do not certify a furnished physical boundary.
