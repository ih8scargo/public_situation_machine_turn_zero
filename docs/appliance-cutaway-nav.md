The front comprises four actual world-space annular faces around a square hole.
Four approach quads connect that hole to the mouth; the left/right quads have
independent, stable IDs. The default lateral flare is 0.4 over depth 2 (about 11.3°
from the depth axis); resizing recomputes the quads. The lower approach differs
because it joins the Floor. There is no claim that these angles are appointed.

The mouth connects to modeled side, ceiling, rear and Floor surfaces. Side/rear/
ceiling center panels are deliberately sectioned out, leaving their perimeter
surfaces so the apparatus can be inspected from either side and behind. Inspection
from below also sections the Floor. This is an open architectural cutaway, not a
sealed room with invisible solid walls. Surface geometry, course, COB and notices
use the same world projector. A projected enclosure-hull clip bounds the apparatus;
the front annulus masks the interior around its actual opening.

The interior support faces form the background layer of this convex cutaway;
world objects are ordered by depth, with the near-facing front annulus as the
opening mask. This avoids the incorrect disappearance of a distant supported road
behind an average-depth Floor. This SVG renderer is not a general solid-geometry
intersection engine; new intersecting rooms would require a stronger visibility
model rather than extending this layering convention blindly.

The native cab is outside the rotating SVG. Its rectangle stays fixed when the
Crossing width, view bearing, course or issuance changes. The desktop shell has a
square reserved extent; narrow screens retain a square projection face and attach

## One finite course, same turn

`Architecture.TraversalCourse` derives a proposal from the saved appointed stick.
Its first point is **exactly the retained COB grasp locality**, named
`pointingable-flick-locality-v1`; the cab Flick is its Human-facing control, not a
second world origin. Appointing the course stores the entire versioned proposal.
No original stick, event, inscription locality or Human FROM is rewritten.

The base course uses eight waypoints and shared cubic Hermite tangents, sampled at
24 intervals per segment (169 samples). It departs along the appointed direction,
descends through broad bends, moves laterally from roughly X −2.1 to X +1.9, and
approaches [0, Floor+0.35, 11.2] along +Z. Each sample retains point, tangent,
banked local up, issue number and cumulative distance. The bank profile is
`1.35 sin(2πu) sin²(πu)` radians (about ±50.2° maximum). This is not a wall-climbing
or contact-physics simulation. Course-local orientation and up also carry the
body rings, four-point grasp and transported tool offset. Selecting a pointing
target swivels the carried tool presentation without steering the course or
rewriting its source bearing/cylinder.

The browser witness's initial course is 14.981 units long, descending from
Y 0.592 to −0.85; other permitted appointments derive their own departure and
Floor. Tests cover both extremes of the existing yaw/pitch purchase range.
The stable destination ID is `cob-traversaling-seat-locality-v1`, named
THIS HERE APPROACHINGING LANDINGING, with retained appointment provenance and the
future **Seat WITH COB** requirement. The ring is a locality marker, not a seat
left behind, a completed embodied Craft, or a seated relation.

NAV advances at two world units/second, using absolute distance, not a fraction
of a changing road length. Forward, reverse, pause, slider arrows, Home/End and
Return COB remain available independently of camera orientation. Arrivals stop
playback at station distances; a further Forward is an explicit Human operation.
Arrival does not sit, seal, Wedding, RE-STEP or RE-ZIPPER.

Additional issuance requires **Appoint return loop**, then **Issue JANUSITE**.
Each issuance appends 96 samples for a bounded smooth return from the marked
locality to that same locality; its departure/closure tangent is +Z. Three such
issues are allowed in this rehearsal. Every existing sample remains byte-for-byte
unchanged, and the original destination stays fixed. No next room is invented.
The cab reports issue count, section count and total length; the map colors the
added extent separately. A loop can be appointed and issued while COB is traveling.
Issuance never advances COB. The future continuation is explicitly appointed even
if it is furnished before arrival.

The persisted `standing.traversal_rehearsal` holds the proposal/appointment, full
course, issuance records, source turn basis, version and optimistic revision.
It does not append constitutional opening events or produce another Human FROM.
Stale revisions and unappointed issuance are rejected; reload retains the course
and issues. **Playback position, view and open inspectors are client-local**;
reload returns the rehearsal COB to its retained departure, without a constitutional
transport or change to the appointed source material.

## Outreading and official notices

The cab's top-down XZ Outreading derives its path, issued portions, Flick, stable
destination, COB dot and direction arrow directly from that same course and pose.
It has no independent motion clock. It can be dismissed/reopened without moving
COB or covering the main projection. It is a Regarding presentation of the held
state, not another accomplished locality.

Four anchored notices at 2%, 48%, 67% and 90% of the initial course cover departure,
banking, NAV versus RE-STEP, and future seating. Their world panels use the sampled
local frame. Click a panel, or Inspect next notice, to read its full front in the
cab; Notice reverse changes only the reader. Each explicitly identifies its
experimental official authority. These are not CH-authored inscriptions. No Doll
pinning, semantical production system or complete inscription mechanism is added.
The retained constitutional XT surfaces still have their convertible flat readers.

## Reproduction and witnesses

1. Open `/experiments/architecture`. Scroll normally to the appliance. PULL using
   the hollow square (keyboard: Enter, ArrowDown, Enter).
2. Squeeze PUFF THROUGH. Feed THROUGH and release FOR COB (Enter, aim with arrows,
   four PageUp presses, Enter). The cab enables the proposed course appointment.
3. Appoint Flick → seat course; press Skate +, Pause and Skate −. The green purchase
   also accepts arrows and Home/End. Return COB returns to the departure.
4. Inspect Front, Left approach, Right approach, Back cutaway, Groundward and Field
   Upside; try THROUGH COB. Change Crossing width. Native instruments remain fixed.
5. Toggle the top-down Outreading; inspect a notice and its reverse. COB stays put.
6. Reach the initial destination without issuing anything. To rehearse more road,
   explicitly appoint a return loop and issue JANUSITE. Continue or reverse across
   it; compare retained track, marker, source appointment and issue counts.
7. Reload the same ID to verify held course/issuance; compare the preserved
   `mode=early` and `mode=retained` references when useful.
