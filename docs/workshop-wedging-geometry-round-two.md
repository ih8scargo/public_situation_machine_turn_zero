## What the geometry demonstrates

One room supplies all four presentations. The original eight plan vertices are retained as X/Z coordinates. Rings connect the framework from Ground Y=0 to the candidate aperture level Y=3; a rising perimeter rail witnesses spiral continuity. HERE square, Landinging circle and approach retain the Round One plan relation. The original octagon has unequal edge lengths; no regular-octagon theorem is asserted.

Each pair starts from **one** HERE-based planar spine with outbound connector, a middle 90-degree elliptical elbow, and return connector. UP-OVER adds a vertical profile; ordinary inherits that spine through profile subtraction and an explicit panel-bearing map. Both centerlines start and return at `[0, radius, 0]`; their tube undersides meet the existing Ground. Each course has 97 sampled sections, 12 vertices per section and 1,152 indexed surface quads.

The plateau elbows have equal sampled centerline curvature after rigid bearing/height transfer (default maximum difference zero). Whole-course 3D curvature differs (default maximum difference approximately 1.9433) because the approach/return lifts differ. Both inherit cross-section radius (`0.04`, hence inverse-radius curvature `25` by default) and section-frame construction. This does **not** prove identical full swept-surface curvature, global bearing curvature or a physical purchase law.

WHAT-FOR UPON ON planes are actual finite horizontal pads at the ordinary elbow's top and the UP-OVER elbow's underside. Their contact-distance witnesses are zero within floating-point tolerance; a triangulated support web connects them. This establishes geometric tangencies and a proposed support relation, not structural load capacity. Visual crossing alone is never used as proof of UPON.

Default same-panel minimum capsule clearance is approximately `0.01099`; next-neighbor approximately `0.07359`; previous-neighbor approximately `-0.00408`. Confirmation requires at least `0.002`, reservation containment and clearance from all existing pairs. Clearance uses segment interiors on the rendered piecewise-linear centerlines, subtracting the two tube radii. It excludes the explicitly shared HERE junction inside planar centerline radius `0.7`; only one Traversaling is active. Mesh vertex containment checks the original room plus selected root wedge and Y=0..3. Junction traffic, support-web interference and whole-structure physical viability remain unverified.

The two Footings are distinct experimental records at the common return locality, **not** two bodies proven three units apart. THREE is measured between existing pillar X extrema; FOUR spans the existing octagon. The interface associates the two returns with the proposed THREE investigation, without claiming a derived three-extent transfer law or executing RE-STEP.
