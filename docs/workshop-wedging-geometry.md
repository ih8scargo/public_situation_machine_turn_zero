## Experiments

- **A · Little ROUNDING Footings.** Follow the continuous octagonal perimeter with one footing witness per bay. Depth zero isolates this course.
- **B · Coordinationingably accretioning.** At each generation a wedge's outer edge becomes two child mouth edges. Each child extends its half outward at a smaller radial increment. Compare depths zero through three. All bays share the same rule and only local growth is drawn.
- **OVER ACROSS Regarding.** Pivot either way through four candidate quarter-turn resolutions. Facing mouth exposes the one directly facing bay. Forward side exposes mouths whose midpoints fall ahead of the transverse line. These are two locality rules on the same plan. Changing the rule or bearing clears a selection if it leaves the exposed set.
- **Selectioning and RE-STANDING.** Select a local bay and compare its inner footing with the outer edge of its first wedge. RE-STANDING places a marker at the footing. The dotted hypothetical RE-STEP span measures the relationship to the outer edge visually. It has no transition control.
- **Inspection.** Rotate the plan drawing independently of the bearing, selection, and standing. The two fixed orthographic elevations project the same four candidate supports. Along X, two supports share a sightline and appear as one.
- **Approach and aperture.** The plan locates the HERE square, THIS ONE STANDINGING LANDINGING circle, approach and overhead opening. The section sketches their vertical relationship.
